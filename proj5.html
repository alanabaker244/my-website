About this collection

Passion, Mostly

As many college students before me (and many after too), I am broke. This means I make do with what I have. I thrifted and repaired an old EOS T100, and scored a lens off a local marketplace.

And I turned it into this. What I have assembled below is a loose congolmeration of my efforts in both coding and photography, so bear with me there.

All the things you find below can be done at home with your hands, some time, and a lot of love for the night.
